What is Toy Town Mayhem?

Toy Town Mayhem in an upcoming first person game with multiple gamemodes.
Players can play first person shooter modes such as Battle Royale or Free For All, as well as more party themed game modes, featuring activities such as tag and floor is lava!
Toy Town Mayhem will be completely free to play, and will be available for Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android!
