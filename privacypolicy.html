Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Account Data

Creating an Account is optional. If You create one, We collect:

  • Email address and password (the password is handled by our account provider, PlayFab)

  • Username

  • The items, cosmetics and tokens You own, and Your purchase history

Gameplay Data

  • Name and outfit: Your username or platform name (for example, Your Steam name) and the outfit You wear are shown to the other players in Your match.

  • Chat: messages You type in the in-game chat are sent to the other players in Your match. We do not store chat messages; only the last few lines are kept in the game while the match is running.

  • Achievements: on Steam, Your achievements are saved to Your Steam account.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service.

Usage Data may include Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), Device type, operating system, a unique Device identifier, how You use the game (for example, which modes and maps You play) and diagnostic data such as errors.

Your IP address is needed to connect You to multiplayer matches. When You play through Steam, matches connect through Steam's relay network, so other players do not see Your IP address.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game saves Your settings, Your selected outfit and Your login on Your Device so You don't have to set them up again. This data stays on Your Device.

Service Providers

We use the following Service Providers to run the game. Each one handles Your data under its own privacy policy:

  • PlayFab (Microsoft): Accounts, logins, owned items and tokens. PlayFab also collects a Device identifier and basic Device information.

  • Alteruna: Multiplayer servers. Alteruna receives Your IP address and the game data needed to run Your match.

  • Steam (Valve): On the Steam version, Your Steam ID, Steam name, achievements and multiplayer connections.

  • Unity Analytics: Usage Data and diagnostic data, used to understand how the game is played and to fix problems.

  • Stores (Steam, Microsoft Store, Google Play, Apple App Store) and Unity In-App Purchasing: Payments for purchases. The store processes Your payment; We never see Your card or payment details.

  • Discord: If Discord is running on Your Device, the game shows what You are doing in the game on Your Discord profile (Rich Presence).
